A letter from Baked with Hope ♡

More than a mini cake.

Baking has been something I always enjoyed doing just for fun, but through personal experiences like my dad being deported and everything happening around us in the world today, I started thinking about how I could turn something I enjoy into something with purpose.

That's when I decided I wanted my business, Baked with Hope, to be more than just desserts. My goal through this business is to raise money through selling my desserts to one day be able to afford for my family and I to reunite with my dad in Vietnam, while also giving back to immigrant families through donations to organizations like RAICES.

Thank you so much for all your love and support, it truly means the world to me.

♡

One cake closer to home.

- Tina

Where donations go

Supporting immigrant families through RAICES.

A portion of Baked with Hope proceeds will be donated to RAICES, an organization that supports immigrant, refugee, and asylum-seeking families through legal aid, social services, and advocacy.

This mission is personal to me, and every order helps turn something sweet into something meaningful.

Order Policy

Placing Your Order 🔗

  • Orders must be placed through the link in my bio.
  • Orders are accepted on a first come, first serve basis.
  • For bulk orders, please send me a message!

Order Availability 📅

  • Baked with Hope is a one-person business, so I will only be accepting up to 10 orders each week.
  • Once I reach my order limit, the order form will close until the next batch of orders opens up, which will be the following week.

Pickup 🚗

  • Pickup is by appointment only during the time and date selected when ordering.
  • If you’re running late to your pickup, please send me a message!

Payments $

  • Payment is due before pickup.
  • Accepted payment methods: Cash, Venmo, Cash App, and Zelle.

Cancellations 🚫

  • If you need to cancel your order or change your pickup date, please let me know as soon as possible.

Allergy Information 🍰

  • If you have any allergies, please let me know before placing your order, and I’ll be happy to answer any questions!

As Baked with Hope is a one-person business, I kindly ask that you please respect my time and my policies. Every order is baked, decorated, packaged, and prepared by me, so I truly appreciate your patience and understanding as I do my best to accommodate everyone 💗🍬
