Order Policy

Placing Your Order 🔗

  • Orders must be placed through the link in my bio.
  • Orders are accepted on a first come, first serve basis.
  • For bulk orders, please send me a message!

Order Availability 📅

  • Baked with Hope is a one-person business, so I will only be accepting up to 10 orders each week.
  • Once I reach my order limit, the order form will close until the next batch of orders opens up, which will be the following week.

Pickup 🚗

  • Pickup is by appointment only during the time and date selected when ordering.
  • If you’re running late to your pickup, please send me a message!

Payments $

  • Payment is due before pickup.
  • Accepted payment methods: Cash, Venmo, Cash App, and Zelle.

Cancellations 🚫

  • If you need to cancel your order or change your pickup date, please let me know as soon as possible.

Allergy Information 🍰

  • If you have any allergies, please let me know before placing your order, and I’ll be happy to answer any questions!

As Baked with Hope is a one-person business, I kindly ask that you please respect my time and my policies. Every order is baked, decorated, packaged, and prepared by me, so I truly appreciate your patience and understanding as I do my best to accommodate everyone 💗🍬
